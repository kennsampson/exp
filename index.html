Using a screen reader?

EXP is built with a game engine, which draws everything onto a canvas: a single picture with no text, headings, or buttons that a screen reader can read. There's no way for assistive technology to describe the town, its people, or their stories.

My regular portfolio covers the same background as a standard website. You can still play with a keyboard here: arrow keys or WASD to move, Space to talk, Enter for the menu.
